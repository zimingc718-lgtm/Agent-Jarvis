NEW Standards Committee (SC) Action to Support Transition to the 
Modernization of Standards Processes and Procedures (MSPP) 
Framework 
 
SC Event Details 
 
At its February 2026 meeting, the NERC Board of Trustees accepted the MSPP Task Force’s 
Recommendations for Transforming the Reliability Standard Development Framework  to 
more effectively and efficiently develop Reliability Standards to meet the speed at which 
risks to the Bulk Power System emerge. Subject to regulatory approval, NERC staff expect 
implementation of the new standards development process framework as early as quarter 
3 or 4 of 2027.

To facilitate an efficient and orderly transition to the new process, the SC took action during its August 19, 2026 meeting to discontinue the following low 
and medium priority standards development projects under the current process, with an intent to rec onsider them under the new standards intake and 
prioritization process envisioned under the MSPP framework: 
• Project 2020-06 Veriﬁcations of Models and Data for Generators 
• Project 2022-02 Uniform Modeling Framework for IBR 
• Project 2023-05 Modiﬁcations to FAC-001 and FAC-002 
• Project 2023-07 Transmission System Planning Performance Requirements for Extreme Weather 
• Project 2023-08 Modiﬁcations of MOD-031 Demand and Energy Data 
• Project 2026-01 PRC-006 Standard Updates 
• Project 2022-04 EMT Modeling 
• Project 2017-01 Modiﬁcations to BAL-003 - Phase II 
• Project 2019-04 Modiﬁcations to PRC-005-6 
• Project 2021-01 System Model Validation with IBRs 
• Project 2021-02 Modiﬁcations to VAR-002-4.1 
• Project 2021-08 Modiﬁcations to FAC-008 
 
Under the new process, the remaining active Standard Authorization Requests for these projects would be submitted as Standard  Initiation Requests 
and evaluated through the MSPP framework to determine whether standards development activities should be initi ated. Impacted project drafting 
teams will work with NERC staff to coordinate next steps.   
 
Additional information on this SC action can be found on the SC event detail page linked above.

REMINDER Small Group Advisory Session s on CIP Standards Addressing 
Virtualization Effective in 2028 
NERC, in collaboration with the Regional Entities, will host a Small Group Advisory Session 
(SGAS) for registered entities to discuss the revised CIP Standard Requirements and 
modiﬁed deﬁnitions addressing Virtualization developed in Standard Drafting Project 2016 
02. In addition to reviewing the changes to CIP Reliability Standards with mandatory 
compliance dates in 2028, the SGAS will also discuss early adoption, “per system capability” 
reporting, and industry questions.

session is not necessary to participate in this general session.

NEW NERC Seeks Comment on MSPP ROP Revisions 
The formal comment period for proposed revisions to the ROP  developed as part of the Modernization of Standards Processes and Procedures (MSPP) 
Initiative is open through October 26 . Stakeholders are invited to review the materials and provide comments on the ROP via this link. Please visit the 
MSPP initiative page  to ﬁnd additional materials and more information on the supporting charter documents and updates to Appendix 3A (Standards 
Processes Manual). 
 
REMINDER NERC Seeks Comment on Computational Loads ROP Revisions 
On August 19, NERC posted proposed revisions to the NERC Rules of Procedure (ROP) Appendix 2, 5A, and 5B addressing computational loads for a 30-day 
comment period. Based on comments received from the April posting of initial proposed criteria, NERC is proposing additional revisions, including two new 
functional entity types, called “Computational Load Owner” and “Computational Load Operator.” Details can be found on the Proposed Revisions to the 
Rules of Procedure page. Comments are due via email on September 18, 2026.
